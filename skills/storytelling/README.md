# Storytelling

**Make the audience understand the idea before you name it.**

Most AI storytelling advice adds drama, polish, or a familiar framework. That can make the writing smoother while making the thinking flatter.

Storytelling is built around a different rule: the narrative should make the audience *earn* the insight.

## What it does

Storytelling shapes the structure of an idea, article, post, talk, executive narrative, or personal story.

It focuses on five things:

1. **Lock the thesis** — decide what the story is actually trying to reveal before the anecdote takes over.
2. **Create a live question** — open with an observation, tension, contradiction, decision, or puzzle that makes the next beat necessary.
3. **Reveal progressively** — let each beat answer one question while creating the next.
4. **Show before name** — make the mechanism understandable before introducing the framework or term.
5. **Earn the ending** — close by changing how the opening is understood, not by attaching a generic moral or call to action.


## Storytelling vs Wordsmith

**Storytelling** decides what happens when, what the audience knows at each point, and how the insight is revealed.

**Wordsmith** improves the sentence once the structure is right.

A useful sequence is:

`Storytelling → Wordsmith`
