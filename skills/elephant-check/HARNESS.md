# HARNESS — Elephant / Human Incentives Reality

Use this harness when the Elephant Check should operate as a hard gate rather than a loose diagnostic.

## Gate A — Actor reality

Identify the actor who controls the relevant decision, the principal they represent, what they are measured on, what authority they possess, and which constraints are binding.

Do not substitute the system designer's objective for the actor's objective.

## Gate B — Behavioral baseline

State what the actor will probably do if the proposed mechanism does not exist.

Distinguish observed or documented behavior from inference. If the baseline depends on a strong behavioral claim with no evidence, label it unsupported.

## Gate C — Resource disposition

For every resource the proposal saves, suppresses, or makes unnecessary, classify the likely disposition:

`eliminated | reallocated | delayed | accelerated | expanded | contracted | defended/withheld | unresolved`

Never allow "saved" to silently mean "disappeared."

## Gate D — Second-order response

Ask how actors adapt once the mechanism exists. Test gaming, budget migration, KPI substitution, channel shifting, strategic withholding, new arbitrage, organizational defense, and competitive response.

## Gate E — Elasticity test

Identify which supposedly fixed inputs can expand or contract and which supposedly flexible behaviors are institutionally fixed within the relevant horizon.

For committed marketing budgets, default to reallocation within the planning horizon unless evidence supports non-spend or contraction.

## Gate F — Revise or kill

Restate the strategy using realistic actor behavior.

If value disappears once realistic reallocation or adaptation is included, kill or narrow the strategy. If it survives, carry the behavioral assumptions explicitly into experiments, architecture, economics, and value-capture design.

## Required execution state

End with exactly one:

- `STRATEGY_SURVIVES_ELEPHANT`
- `REALLOCATION_REQUIRED`
- `ELASTICITY_UNRESOLVED`
- `BEHAVIOR_ASSUMPTION_UNSUPPORTED`
- `STRATEGY_FAILS_ELEPHANT`

## Minimal output template

`Actor | Actual objective/incentive | Baseline behavior | Freed resource | Likely disposition | Second-order response | Elasticity | Revised mechanism | Execution state`
